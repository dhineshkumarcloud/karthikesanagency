    About Us

    About Karthikesan Agencies

    Karthikesan Agencies is a well-established FMCG distribution company based in Karaikal – TR Pattinam. We specialize in supplying a comprehensive range of consumer goods including soft drinks, packaged drinking water, snacks, and household products to retailers and businesses across the region.

    With years of hands-on experience in the distribution industry, we have built a reputation for reliable supply chains, on-time delivery, and competitive pricing. Our deep understanding of the local market allows us to anticipate demand and ensure that our retail partners never face stock shortages.

    We believe in building long-term relationships based on trust, consistency, and mutual growth. Whether you are a small retailer or a large business, Karthikesan Agencies is your dependable distribution partner.
